FAQ

Frequently asked.

Where do the leads come from?▼

Our AI scans dozens of official Florida government databases and public record sources every day. We monitor new business registrations, property transactions, professional license filings, permit applications, and other life events that create insurance needs. The data comes directly from state and county government systems.

How fresh are the leads?▼

Our pipeline runs every morning. Most leads are 1-3 days old from the original filing date. Some sources update within 24 hours. You're contacting people during their decision-making window, not weeks or months after the fact.

Are the leads exclusive to me?▼

Exclusivity depends on your plan, and is scoped per insurance category. Pro plan: You are the only agent receiving that lead record for that insurance category. Growth plan: Semi-exclusive. Starter plan: Shared. All plans cover 1 FL county of your choice. See Terms, Section 7a for the full per-category rule.

What contact info is included?▼

Each lead includes: name, company (if business), address, city, county, and ZIP. Phone and email are included when publicly available. Business filings typically include registered agent contact information. Coverage varies by source. We also provide AI lead scoring, insurance category tags, and ready-to-use call scripts with every lead.

What's your refund policy?▼

Every new subscription comes with 10 bonus leads delivered instantly. Subscriptions are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime. No contracts, no cancellation fees.

Can I pay with Bitcoin?▼

Yes. We accept Bitcoin and Lightning Network payments. You get a 10% discount on any subscription when paying with Bitcoin.

How is this different from open-market leads?▼

Traditional lead vendors sell aged, recycled internet leads to 5-8 agents at $25-50 each. Our leads are sourced directly from government records, often within 24 hours of filing. They're based on real life events, not ad clicks, so you're reaching people who actually need insurance right now.
